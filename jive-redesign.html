About Jive

Built on passion,
proven by performance

Jive Entertainment started with one speaker and a belief that every event deserves great sound. Today we are the Eastern Cape's trusted partner for weddings, corporate functions, and school events.

We do not subcontract. Our team arrives early, tests everything twice, and stays until the last guest leaves. That is why 90% of our business comes from repeat clients and referrals.
